# Vex picture folder

Put pictures you downloaded for your topics in this folder.
They stay on your laptop (git ignores everything here except this file).

## How to name a picture
Use the topic in the name, lowercase, words joined by dashes:

    levelling-staff-and-dumpy-level.png
    setting-out-with-profile-boards.jpg
    bearing-of-a-beam-and-supports.svg

Accepted types: .png .jpg .jpeg .webp .svg

## Where to get pictures
- Your own photos and your guideline/textbook pages (best: they match your syllabus).
- Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org): free, open licences, no key needed.
  Check the licence on each file page; if it says CC BY, note the author.
- Do not use pictures from sites that forbid reuse.

## Source note (optional, one line per picture)
Create `sources.txt` here and add: `filename | where it came from | licence`.
Boards will show this as the picture credit once pictures are wired in.
